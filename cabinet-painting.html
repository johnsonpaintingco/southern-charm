Our approach

Cabinet Painting planned around your project

Every property is different. We start by understanding the space, surface conditions, your expectations, and the scope of work before recommending a practical next step.

Start with the cabinet condition

The suitability of painting depends on the existing finish, cabinet material, damage, and how the doors and drawers are used.

A process built around preparation

A professional cabinet scope may include labeling and removing doors, cleaning, surface preparation, bonding primer, suitable cabinet coatings, and careful reassembly as agreed.

Review color and finish options

Consider lighting, countertops, flooring, hardware, and the desired sheen when planning the cabinet update.
